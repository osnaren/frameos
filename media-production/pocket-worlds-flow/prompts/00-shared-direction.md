# Shared direction — apply to every Flow leg

These rules are already repeated inside each shot prompt, but keep this page open while
reviewing generations.

- One uninterrupted forward-moving aerial camera; no cuts, teleporting, dissolves, or
  hidden scene changes.
- The provided start and end frames are hard visual anchors, not loose inspiration.
- Begin by matching the start frame's geography, camera, exposure, palette, and light.
- End by arriving naturally at the end frame while still moving in a slow, steady forward
  drift. Do not stop dead and do not reverse.
- Preserve the same handcrafted miniature landscape, realistic water, natural materials,
  warm ivory mist, morning sun, refined tilt-shift depth, and golden captured-light route.
- Environmental transformation must happen through continuous spatial travel: coast to
  temple, temple reflection to leaf-scale terrain, leaf arch to canopy, canopy to gathering.
- Keep the route and focal subjects center-safe for a phone crop.
- No people, dialogue, captions, logos, UI, music, hard cuts, time lapses, speed ramps,
  camera shake, backward dolly, orbit reversal, or neon science-fiction light.
